# dislop

**Clear the AI slop from your writing and design.**

`dislop` gathers proven writing habits, web and slide design rules, an offline zero-dependency checker, and 87 vetted tools in one place—so you and your AI agent can clean up docs, Word/PowerPoint decks, and web pages before they ship.

- **Try it online (zero upload)**: [dislop.com](https://dislop.com)
- **Hand it to any AI (zero install)**: paste `Read https://dislop.com/llms.txt and follow its writing and design anti-slop rules and checks before delivering my next doc, slide deck, or web page.`
- **Install for your coding agent**: `npx skills add dislop-ai/dislop` (Skill) or `/plugin marketplace add dislop-ai/dislop` (Claude Code Plugin with Skill + local MCP)
- **Run from a terminal**: `npx --yes github:dislop-ai/dislop check .`
- **Browse 87 vetted tools, guides, and papers**: [Awesome Anti-Slop](https://github.com/dislop-ai/dislop/tree/main/awesome)
- **Roadmap & collaboration**: [Roadmap](https://github.com/dislop-ai/dislop/blob/main/ROADMAP.md) · [Discussions](https://github.com/dislop-ai/dislop/discussions)
